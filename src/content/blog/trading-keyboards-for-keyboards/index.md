A few people have asked where the music went now that the old weblogs.asp.net blog is no longer available (Amir got me to put this together). I used to post songs there for fun, so I’m putting them here for historical sake (and because it’s still fun to look back on). You’ll find the later studio tracks first, then some more cheesy demos from my 1980s recording studio days below. A couple of those are tape recordings off the radio from when [Family of Friends](#family-of-friends) actually got played on pop stations. Fun times!

Fair warning: some of these are more polished, some are rough demos, and a few should probably have stayed in a box. 😀 That said, here they are.
